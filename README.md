# Hi, I’m Koen

I’m a Marketing Management student at the University of Guelph, a web developer, and the founder of a student-run window cleaning business. I got into building software by running into problems I wanted to solve myself. I like talking to the people who actually use a tool, figuring out what slows them down, and building something that makes their day easier.

### What I’m interested in

Most of my projects use TypeScript, React, and Next.js. I’m especially interested in commerce, product analytics, and AI tools that make messy information easier to act on. Running a service business keeps me honest about what makes software useful: it has to help someone get something done.
